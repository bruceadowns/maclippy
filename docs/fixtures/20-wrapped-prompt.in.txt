  Three readings, and they don't cost the same:

  1. xtransfer* (high side). DataSync publishes directly into the cross-domain broker, on the exact topics you repartition irreversibly. This is the collision case, and the one AC-27 is written for.
  2. crossdomain* (low side). DataSync publishes low-side, NiFi carries it across, it arrives on xtransfer* keyed by whatever the high-side processor sets — so the key contract holds transitively. But the low-side path is explicitly
     Out of Scope in your DRP, and the low-side producer sends an empty-string key today, so "transitively" is doing real work there.
  3. Neither — the author means the mechanism, not a topic. DataSync already has its own broker cluster, and the epic pairs the phrase with "or whether an alternative solution better fits the requirements." That's an evaluation of
     whether to use the cross-domain transfer path at all, with topic selection downstream. "Direct to cross-transfer or via handler" is then a routing choice inside that mechanism.
