The requirement

Idempotency is a hard requirement on the pipeline, not on each stage. Individual stages needn't be idempotent alone; only the composition must be. Most are trivially so - flatten emits no character it
also consumes, dedent leaves a common prefix of zero.

Two reasons it's non-negotiable here:

Nothing stops a second click. There's no UI state saying "already reformatted." A non-idempotent transform degrades the text a little more each time, and since capture dedupes by verbatim plain, it also
spawns a new history clip per click instead of bumping the existing one. §3's "second Reformat is a free no-op" depends entirely on this property holding.

It's the category convention. gofmt, Black and Prettier all guarantee it explicitly. It's the cheapest invariant in text transforms and catches the most.
